MISSION STATEMENT

From single-use waste to secondary resource.

ReLoop connects consumers, collection centres, and manufacturing companies in a unified digital ledger. By providing tangible rewards and full traceability, ReLoop incentivizes segregation at source and ensures high-volume clean plastics reach registered circular recyclers.

STEP 01

Return & Segregate

Citizens drop clean post-consumer packaging at registered campus and neighborhood collection hubs.

STEP 02

Batch & Consolidate

Collection centres verify material purity and pool homogeneous plastic into 20 KG pickup batches.

STEP 03

Recover & Reward

Industrial recyclers receive the shipments and trigger automated demonstration milestone reward points.

Academic Prototype Notice

ReLoop is a conceptual academic demonstration system. Actual transport logistics, recycling facilities, and legal extended producer responsibility (EPR) compliance would require official operational agreements with registered waste management authorities.
